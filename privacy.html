Gratwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Gratwell (“the app”) is a tip and hours tracker for iPhone, published by Akeem Rivera (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information. We run no servers for the app.
  • The app works offline. There is no account and nothing to sign in to.
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics or advertising tools.
  • What you enter (jobs, shifts, hours, tips, wages, notes and settings) is stored on your iPhone, and in backups you choose to keep.

Information stored on your device

Your records are kept in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see them.

Backups

  • iCloud backup: the app can save backup copies to your own iCloud Drive (Files › iCloud Drive › Gratwell) using your Apple Account. Apple stores them under your agreement with Apple. We cannot see or access them.
  • Backup files: you can save a backup file wherever you choose with the share sheet (for example, Save to Files) and restore from it later. Those files go only where you send them.

Imports and exports

  • When you import a CSV file, the app reads it on your device only.
  • CSV exports and PDF summaries go only where you send them.

Permissions (each is optional)

  • Face ID: only to unlock the app when App Lock is on. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never receives your face data.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on. They are scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Gratwell Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase (currently $19.99 in the U.S.; the App Store shows the price in your region) handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Links

Some screens can open an outside website (for example, the U.S. Department of Labor) in your browser when you tap a link. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or delete the app. Backup and export files stay where you saved them until you delete them. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete or send you on request.

Children

Gratwell is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone’s passcode, encryption and app sandboxing, and optionally by App Lock. Keep your device, Apple Account and any exported files secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date and post the new version here.
