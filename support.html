Gratwell Support

Gratwell is an offline tip and hours tracker for iPhone. There’s no account, so there’s nothing to sign in to.

Questions or problems? Email stemwyn@mail.grokbot.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the Gratwell version. Please don’t send screenshots that show card numbers or customer names.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

  • Gratwell can save a daily backup to your own iCloud Drive and keeps the last 7 days. On a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, open Backup & restore to restore one. New backups can take a few minutes to appear.
  • You can also save a backup file anywhere with “Save a backup file” and restore from it later. You’ll see what’s in a backup before anything changes.

Gratwell Pro

  • Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Apple, not a subscription. If you reinstall or change phones, use Restore purchase in the app.
  • For refunds, use Apple’s reportaproblem.apple.com.
